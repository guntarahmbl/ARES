Sources & assumptions

Eight source products are locally integrated. HiRISE/CTX terrain and imagery, USGS geology, CRISM, PLACES, surface photographs and MEDA. Routing metrics are derived; habitat and EVA resources are assumed.

Calculation assumptions

Mars equirectangular coordinates, radius 3396190 m; center 77.405123636° E, 18.451193185° N. Local x east, z south. 81 × 81 grid, 5 m spacing. Deterministic least-cost search, 8-connected grid without diagonal corner cutting. Excluded and nodata cells cannot be crossed.

Known limitations

Habitat and EVA activities are hypothetical. Geology has 100–200 m registration uncertainty and is not used for navigation. CRISM indicators have native 18 m resolution and are not confirmed mineral detections. Photos are localized by camera site/drive, not image footprint. The return envelope describes this model only; it does not establish real-world safety. MEDA sols 307–313 are earlier historical observations, not a forecast; weather and radiation do not modify routing.

Reserve applies to both original energy capacity and total EVA time. Lost energy reduces remaining resources. Emergency routing minimizes estimated energy; its time and reserve are checked separately. Speed, energy, science and safety scores are illustrative.
